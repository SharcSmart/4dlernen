### Electric SUV Range in 2026: Real-World Performance

When evaluating which electric SUVs offer the most utility for long-distance driving in 2026, real-world range is determined by a combination of battery efficiency, aerodynamics, and charging speed.

#### Efficiency vs. Comfort
In the mid-size SUV segment, the choice often comes down to how a vehicle manages energy at motorway speeds. 

*   **Tesla Model Y:** Typically leads in outright efficiency. For drivers whose primary concern is covering the maximum distance on motorways with the least amount of energy, the Model Y remains a frequent choice due to its software integration and charging network maturity.
*   **BMW iX3 and Audi Q4 e-tron:** These models focus on a conventional premium experience. While the iX3 is optimized for daily commuting and family duties, it is designed to remain quiet and composed at motorway speeds, which can impact how drivers perceive range during long-distance travel.

#### Maximizing Range on Long Trips
In 2026, the "usable" range of an electric SUV is often more important than its theoretical maximum. To maintain efficiency during long-distance travel, consider the following strategy:

*   **The 10-80% Window:** Charging is fastest when the battery is between 10% and 80%. Because the charging rate slows significantly for the final 20%, planning for two shorter stops is generally faster than waiting for a single 100% charge.
*   **Efficiency at Speed:** While the Tesla Model Y often wins on raw efficiency, the BMW iX3 is noted for its interior materials and quietness at speed, which may suit drivers who prioritize the quality of the drive over the absolute distance between stops.
*   **Infrastructure:** Real-world range is heavily influenced by the availability of rapid chargers. The Model Y benefits from an established network, whereas the iX3 and Q4 e-tron rely on the broader public rapid-charging infrastructure.
